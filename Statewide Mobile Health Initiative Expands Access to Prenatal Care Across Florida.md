**Statewide Mobile Health Initiative Expands Access to Prenatal Care Across Florida**
=====================================================================================

Simply Healthcare Plans and Healthcare Network have launched a joint statewide initiative aimed at broadening [prenatal care access](https://healthtechinsiders.com/prenatal-care-access-expands-across-florida/) for women in underserved and rural communities across Florida. Utilizing Healthcare Network’s Van Domelen Health Express mobile unit, the project brings direct maternal medical services, health education, and essential social support directly into local neighborhoods. The mobile clinic tour will travel from South Florida through the Panhandle through May 2027, focusing on regions where traditional healthcare facilities remain difficult to reach.

The mobile unit offers essential clinical services at no cost, including pregnancy screenings, non-diagnostic ultrasounds, and comprehensive prenatal and postnatal health guidance. Beyond medical checkups, staff assist families by connecting them with critical non-clinical resources, such as housing assistance, food support, and local transportation options. Local community health centers coordinate patient outreach and appointment scheduling to ensure ongoing medical support remains available long after the mobile unit transitions to its next location.

**Reversing Florida's Declining Early Prenatal Care Rates**
-----------------------------------------------------------

The statewide project comes as health agencies address noticeable declines in early pregnancy interventions throughout Florida. Recent statistics from the Florida Department of Health highlight that first-trimester prenatal care rates dropped from 76.1% in 2020 down to 68.9% in 2024. Early medical visits allow physicians and clinical specialists to monitor maternal and fetal development, detect potential risks early, and provide timely interventions that significantly improve birth outcomes.

The initiative aligns directly with the Florida Agency for Health Care Administration’s (AHCA) "From the Start" program. This state framework emphasizes expanding mobile obstetric services and strengthening care coordination across rural and medically underserved populations. By delivering care directly into vulnerable neighborhoods, the program helps eliminate travel and financial obstacles that frequently delay essential medical attention.

**Strengthening Local Healthcare Infrastructure and Continuity of Care**
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Organizers emphasize that mobile clinics are designed to supplement, rather than replace, existing local healthcare infrastructure. Community health workers and health centers lead patient engagement efforts, establishing trusted connections between patients and regional Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs).

By establishing a reliable link between mobile outreach and permanent community health providers, the initiative creates a sustainable pathway for continuous maternal care. Medical leaders note that overcoming systemic barriers to early prenatal services is vital for reducing healthcare disparities, safeguarding expectant mothers, and ensuring healthier long-term developmental outcomes for infants statewide.
